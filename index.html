Good To Know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install anything?

No — it runs entirely in your browser, on any device.

Can two people book the same slot?

No. Once a slot is pending or approved, it's locked for everyone else until it's freed up.

What if my account gets flagged?

Being flagged doesn't block you — it just means an admin will take a closer look before approving requests.

Who can approve reservations?

Only accounts with the Administrator role can approve, reject, or manage rooms and users.

Can I change my password later?

Yes — every account, including admins, can update their password anytime from the dashboard.
