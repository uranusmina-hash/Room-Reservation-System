Good To Know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install anything?

No — it runs entirely in your browser, on any device.

Can two people book the same slot?

No. Once a slot is pending or approved, it's locked for everyone else until it's freed up.

How do I get an account?

Accounts are created by the school admin for teachers, staff, and student leaders. Ask the admin office for your login.

Who can approve reservations?

Only accounts with the Administrator role can approve, reject, or manage rooms and users.

Can I change my email or password?

Yes — open the Profile tab in your dashboard to update either one. Forgot your password? Use the link on the login page to send a reset request to the admin.
